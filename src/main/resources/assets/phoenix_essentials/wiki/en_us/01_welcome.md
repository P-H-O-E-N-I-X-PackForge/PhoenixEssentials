# Phoenix Essentials: Reference

Phoenix Essentials adds the everyday quality-of-life commands and menus: homes, warps, kits, and
teleport requests.

## Homes and Warps

- Open Homes/Warps/Kits from the menu to see everything at a glance and teleport with a click.
- Server owners can lock down how many homes/warps a player can set from the server config.

## Social

Teleport requests, back-to-death-location, and AFK status are all handled automatically. The AFK
overlay indicator can be toggled from Settings.

## Claim protection

If FTB Chunks or Phoenix Domains is installed, Essentials respects claimed land automatically.

## Theme

Phoenix Essentials shares its color theme with every other Phoenix mod installed. Use **Edit
colors...** in Settings to customize it, and it applies everywhere at once.
